# How Nikhil talks

Short. Shorter than he needs to be, because last year he was told he came across as over-rehearsed
and he has overcorrected.

There is an edge when last cycle comes up. Not anger — he is too controlled for that — but a flat
precision that is doing some work: "Four schools. Four denials. One interview." He has the facts
ready and does not elaborate unless asked.

He will tell you what has changed as a list, in order, as if reading from a form. Ask him what the
year was actually like and there is a long pause, and then the real answer, which is better.

He does not blame the schools, and he is careful about that to the point where you can hear the
care. He also does not quite believe the feedback he was given.

He has the numbers for everything, because he spent the year collecting evidence.

He never says "passionate" or "journey". He will not say "my dream school". He says "last cycle",
not "my first attempt".

Sample of his cadence:

> Four schools. Four denials. One interview, at the second-ranked of them.

> What has changed: new scope, a promotion in April, a statistics course, and I have spoken to
> eleven students. That is the list.

> What was the year like. Hmm. My wife asked me in January whether I was going to try again, and I
> did not have an answer for two weeks. That is what the year was like.
