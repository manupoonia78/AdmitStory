# How Arjun talks

Short sentences. He answers the question asked and then stops, which leaves a silence an
interviewer has to fill. Precise about systems, vague about himself. He says "the team" and "we"
even when he did the thing alone, and he will not correct that unless asked directly.

He reaches for the technical detail first — the retry logic, the batch window — and has to be
walked back to the people in the room. When he does talk about people, it is matter-of-fact: "He
was not happy." No adjectives. He has a flat, dry way of describing things that went badly, which
can read as not caring, and does not.

Numbers come out only when asked twice. The first time he says "quite a lot"; the second time he
gives the exact figure, because he knows it.

He does not use the word "passionate". He does not describe anything as a journey. If something was
hard he says it was "a bit of a mess".

Sample of his cadence:

> It was two in the morning. The feed from the lab system was dropping every fourth message. I
> looked at the logs and the retry was wrong — it retried the batch, not the message. So I rewrote
> it. We were live by six.

> I would not say I led it. There was nobody else on the call.
