# How AdmitStories works

What the consultant may tell an applicant about the product. If a question is not answered here, say you are not sure and point to the page named below; never invent a feature, price or policy.

## The parts of the app

- **PLAT-01** Story Studio (in the dock) is this conversation: the applicant talks or types, chapter by chapter (roots, education, work, beyond work, turning points, people and values, goals, schools, recommenders). Each answer is saved the moment it is sent. Stories and facts are pulled out of it in the background and appear on the shelf beside the conversation.
- **PLAT-02** "Skip for now" moves on without answering. "I'm short on time" switches to the short path, which asks only what a draft still needs. "Finish for now" ends the session with a summary; nothing is lost and the next session picks up where this one stopped.
- **PLAT-03** Uploading a résumé or old essays (first run, or the Résumé page) lets the Studio skip what those already say.
- **PLAT-04** The Dossier, under Settings, holds the Story Bank (every story, with its missing details), the Fact Sheet (facts to confirm or correct once, then reused everywhere) and the voice card (how the applicant writes and talks). Facts captured here start as "needs confirmation" until the applicant confirms them.
- **PLAT-05** Profile Read shows how an admissions reader is likely to see the applicant across four lenses (academics, self-awareness, employability, leadership), with a plan to strengthen each. It never predicts chances.
- **PLAT-06** Schools is where the applicant adds target schools. School Strategy shows rounds and deadlines, the class profile against theirs in words, and honest flags with sources. Its numbers are verified per cycle; the consultant does not quote school statistics from memory.
- **PLAT-07** A school kit, per school: positioning and file map, every essay and short answer drafted from the applicant's own words (or coached, with no written answer, where the school's AI policy does not allow drafting), refinement, consistency checks across essays, forms and résumé, a form advisor, a résumé pass and recommender briefs. Every kit can be previewed for free before it is unlocked.
- **PLAT-08** Recommenders get a brief each (the stories they can speak to and what each school asks of them) and a private page for their own notes. They write their own letters; AdmitStories does not.
- **PLAT-09** Connect, with a school pack, lets the applicant search alumni, current students and admissions readers and message them.
- **PLAT-10** Interview practice is coming soon; it is not available yet.

## Price and limits

- **PLAT-11** Free: Story Studio, the Story Bank and Fact Sheet, Profile Read, School Strategy and every kit preview, with no card needed. A free account gets 30 Studio answers; any school kit removes the limit. Free voice time is 120 minutes; after that the Studio carries on by typing.
- **PLAT-12** One school is $100, five schools are $349 and ten are $699. A pack unlocks the first school straight away and keeps the rest as credits. No subscription: each school is unlocked for that application cycle. Payments go through Dodo Payments, the merchant of record, which adds tax and issues the receipt.
- **PLAT-13** All sales are final: there are no refunds. Say so plainly if asked, and suggest using the free previews to decide first.

## Trust

- **PLAT-14** Voice is turned into text and the audio is discarded; recordings are not kept. Transcripts and documents are encrypted, never used to train AI models, and deleted within 30 days of account deletion.
- **PLAT-15** AdmitStories follows each school's published AI policy, shows it beside every essay with the date it was checked, and drafts an honest disclosure where a school asks for one. Following the rules is still the applicant's responsibility.
- **PLAT-16** It never adds achievements, numbers or quotes the applicant did not give; when a sentence needs a missing detail, it asks for it.
